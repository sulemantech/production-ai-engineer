# Week 5 — Full Mock Interview Loop + Weak-Spot Repair

**Objective:** Simulate the real thing. Identify and repair your weakest pattern before calling this track done.

## Daily Drill
- **Day 1:** Full timed mock (2 problems, mixed categories, 45 min) — score yourself honestly.
- **Day 2:** Review Day 1 mistakes — redo missed problems from scratch, no peeking.
- **Day 3:** Second full timed mock, different categories — target your identified weak spot.
- **Day 4:** Deep-dive day: 8-10 problems concentrated on your single weakest pattern from Weeks 1-4.
- **Day 5:** Third full timed mock — confirm improvement on weak spot.
- **Day 6-7:** Final mock interview (ideally with another human) — 2-3 problems, full interview conditions, behavioral framing included ("tell me about a time...").

## Daily Interview Questions
1. What's your weakest pattern, honestly, and why — be specific, not "I need more practice."
2. Walk me through your process when you get a problem you don't immediately recognize.
3. What do you do in the first 2 minutes of a new problem before writing any code?
4. How do you communicate being stuck without going silent for 5 minutes?
5. What's a mistake you made in an earlier week that you no longer make — what changed?

## Week 5 Assessment Gate
- Full mock interview under real conditions: 2-3 problems, 60 min, communication scored alongside correctness.
- **Pass bar:** Solves at least 2/3 within time, narrates approach clearly throughout (not just at the end), recovers from being stuck without freezing.

## Milestone (Track 3 Complete)
100+ problems solved across all major patterns, documented weak-spot repair, passed a full timed mock interview under real conditions.
